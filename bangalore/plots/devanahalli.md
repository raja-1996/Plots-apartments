# Devanahalli Plots — Builder Projects for Investment (2026)

> Compiled: May 2026. Prices and possession dates are indicative; verify with builders directly before transacting.

## Why Devanahalli?

Devanahalli, in North Bengaluru, is one of the fastest-growing real-estate corridors in the city — anchored by Kempegowda International Airport (15–20 km away) and a cluster of large-scale infrastructure and industrial catalysts.

**Key growth drivers**
- **STRR (NH-948A)** — 280.8 km Satellite Town Ring Road by NHAI under Bharatmala, encircling Bengaluru and connecting 12 satellite towns including Devanahalli.
- **KIADB Aerospace Park** — 847 acres, operational, with HAL, DRDO units, and Boeing's India hub.
- **Aerospace & Defence Park** (2,500 acres) and **Aero SEZ** (3,000 acres).
- **Foxconn iPhone 17 facility** — commenced production August 2025 (~$2.8 B investment).
- **Devanahalli Business Park** and upcoming **metro extension** to the airport.

**Price trajectory**
- 2019: ~₹3,200 /sq.ft
- 2020: ~₹3,500 /sq.ft
- 2026: ~₹8,200–8,500 /sq.ft (premium projects)
- 2027 (forecast): ₹10,000+ /sq.ft for premium projects
- Plots appreciated **35–40% between 2022–2025**, with expected **15–20% annual appreciation through 2027–28**.
- Rental yields from plotted/villa developments: **3–5%**.

## Investment Checklist Before Buying

1. **RERA registration** — verify on Karnataka RERA portal; cross-check the project's registration number (e.g., Provident Deansgate: PR/150323/005796).
2. **Khata** — confirm A-Khata (preferred) vs B-Khata. Most reputed builders deliver A-Khata.
3. **Title & encumbrance** — 30-year title trace + EC from sub-registrar.
4. **Approvals** — BMRDA / BIAAPA / DTCP approval depending on location.
5. **Conversion certificate** — DC conversion (agri → residential) is mandatory.
6. **NA (Non-Agricultural) status** — verify.
7. **Layout plan sanction** — from competent authority.
8. **Possession & registration timeline** — written commitment from builder.
9. **Maintenance & amenities** — corpus, annual charges, association formation timeline.
10. **Resale comparables** — check secondary-market quotes on 99acres / NoBroker / Housing for the same layout.

## Risk Factors

- Many "builder" layouts in outer Devanahalli are still **agricultural conversions in progress** — avoid without DC conversion.
- Some peripheral plotted layouts price near ₹2,800 /sq.ft but lack road / sewerage / clubhouse delivery.
- Possession for several upcoming plotted projects falls in **mid-2026 to 2030** — factor in construction-risk window if you intend to build immediately.
