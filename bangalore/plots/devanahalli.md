## 1. Verification status

This report was assembled by:

1. Running 6 broad web searches across builder, project, macro, and risk angles.
2. Triaging 30 candidate URLs into 6 batches of 5 and dispatching parallel verification subagents that used `WebFetch` to extract structured data from each page.
3. Reconciling subagent findings, prioritising official builder + RERA-verifiable claims.

**Source-fetch yield (important caveat):** Several reputable property portals (99acres, SquareYards, Houssed, Tradebrains, AddressAdvisors) returned HTTP 403 to the WebFetch user-agent, so a number of cross-checks could only be done against initial search-engine snippets rather than full page content. Where corroboration is thin, this report explicitly marks the claim ⚠️ Medium or ❓ Conflict and tells you which lone source supports it.

Confidence tags used:

- ✅ **High** — agreed across 2+ independent sources or RERA-verifiable (registration number visible).
- ⚠️ **Medium** — single source, or only aggregator/SEO pages corroborate.
- ❓ **Conflict** — sources disagree; both values shown.

**Every RERA registration number** below should be verified independently at <https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/> before transacting. A registration number alone does not certify that current project status, possession dates, or carpet area match the live RERA filing.

## 6. Due diligence checklist (Devanahalli plotted developments)

### RERA (Karnataka)

- ✅ Verify the project on <https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/> by registration number — do not rely on the number being printed in a brochure.
- ⚠️ Per single-source data (RealEstateIndia blog): RERA registration is mandatory for plotted projects above 500 sq.m **OR** with 8+ units. Confirm thresholds independently.
- ✅ The Karnataka RERA filing will show the most recent declared possession date, financial disclosures, and complaint history — read the filing, not the brochure.

### Land status

- ✅ Demand the **DC Conversion certificate** (agricultural-to-residential, signed by Deputy Commissioner). Without DC conversion, the land is technically agricultural and individual plot building approvals will be obstructed.
- ✅ Demand the **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)**. One verified source recommended a 30-year EC scan via the Kaveri portal; at minimum, the standard 13-year EC should be drawn for every survey number in the layout.
- ⚠️ For Devanahalli specifically, plots fall under the **BIAPPA** (Bangalore International Airport Area Planning Authority) jurisdiction, not BBMP / BDA. Confirm BIAPPA layout approval order is on file — none of the verified sources in this batch explicitly named BIAPPA, but it is the established planning authority for the airport region. **Verify with a local lawyer.**
- ✅ Prefer **DC-converted, layout-approved, RERA-registered** plots over revenue / gram-panchayat sites. Revenue sites are cheaper but carry materially higher documentation and bankability risk.
- ⚠️ For Khata: A-Khata is preferable for resale + bank loan eligibility; B-Khata indicates incomplete regularisation. (Definitions and Devanahalli applicability were inconsistently covered by sources — verify locally.)

### Site / physical

- ⚠️ Monsoon water-logging window (Jul–Sep) and groundwater depth (cited as 400–500 ft by one source) should be physically inspected.
- ⚠️ Layouts within 5 km of the Devanahalli Wildlife Sanctuary may require forest-department clearance — verify with the seller (single-source flag, but easy to check on the ground).
- ✅ Verify the survey sketch (Tippani) physically matches plot boundaries on site.

### Financial / regulatory

- ✅ Confirm the plot is **bank-loan-eligible** — major banks (HDFC, SBI, LIC HF) maintain approved-project lists; presence on a major lender's list is a strong indirect title check.
- ✅ For under-construction plotted projects, confirm **70% escrow** compliance (a RERA mandate referenced in source).
- ✅ Stamp duty + registration: budget ~6.6–7% of registration value on top of the plot price in Karnataka. Confirm current rates at registration time.

## 7. Risk factors

| Risk | Severity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Possession-date slippage** | Medium-High | Multiple projects above show 1–2 year gaps between brochure dates and stated RERA dates. Read the RERA filing, not the marketing material. |
| **Unverifiable macro claims** | Medium | The "20%+ CAGR on plots" / "₹2.8B Foxconn investment" / "8–15% annual appreciation" figures circulating in promotional content **did not cross-verify** in this audit. Treat as bullish narrative, not financial assumption. |
| **Liquidity / exit risk for plots** | Medium | Plots do not generate rent. Holding cost = opportunity cost + property tax. Plan a 3–7 year horizon minimum for the appreciation thesis to play out. |
| **Layout-approval risk** | Medium-High | BIAPPA / BMRDA approvals for new layouts can be challenged; demolition + loan-rejection risk for unapproved layouts is real per multiple sources. |
| **Builder phase substitution** | Medium | Several major projects (Brigade Oasis, Godrej Reserve, Sumadhura Panorama) sell in 2–5 phases — verify which RERA filing applies to the specific plot quoted. |
| **Internet-listing inaccuracy** | High | Brigade Oasis sources varied between 40 and 70 acres for the same project; Godrej Reserve sources varied between 91.66 and 95.7 acres. SEO pages aggressively rewrite each other and lag the live project. |
| **Stale possession dates on builder pages** | Medium | Sobha Chartered Woodpecker still shows a 2021 possession date on Sobha's own page — read this as a flag to call the builder before assuming current status. |
| **Foxconn / iPhone production claim** | Medium | Repeated heavily in promotional content; the $2.8B figure and Aug 2025 commencement date were **not corroborated** in any full-page fetch. Useful as a directional employment thesis only. |
| **Metro Blue Line timing** | Medium | "Upcoming" is the consistent description — no commissioning date verifiable in sources. Do not underwrite returns assuming a specific Metro opening year. |
