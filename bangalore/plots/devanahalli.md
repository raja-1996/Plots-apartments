## 1. Verification status

This report was assembled by:

1. Running ~11 web searches across builder, project, macro, and risk angles.
2. Triaging ~45 candidate URLs into 9 batches of 5 and dispatching parallel verification subagents that used `WebFetch` to extract structured data from each page.
3. Reconciling subagent findings, prioritising RERA-verifiable claims with the project-format `PR/` prefix (not the agent `AG/` prefix).

**Two critical caveats to read before any project section:**

1. **Aggregator-RERA contamination.** Several aggregator microsites (notably `homznspace.com`) display **the same agent-registration number** — `PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/AG/170824/000174` (and the closely-related `PRM/KA/RERA/1251/311/AG/170824/000174`) — in the footer of *multiple* unrelated project pages. This is the **marketing channel partner's** Karnataka RERA agent number, **not the project's promoter RERA**. Where this report flags an RERA number as "agent-RERA only / not project-RERA", treat it as if no RERA were available on that page until you confirm the genuine project filing on the Karnataka RERA portal.
2. **Many aggregator pages 403'd WebFetch** (99acres, SquareYards, Houssed, AddressAdvisors, Tradebrains). Where the only fetched source was a microsite, this report says so explicitly.

**Confidence tags used:**

- ✅ **High** — agreed across 2+ independent sources or has a valid project-RERA (`PR/` prefix, verifiable on rera.karnataka.gov.in).
- ⚠️ **Medium** — single source, or only aggregator/SEO pages corroborate, or RERA missing.
- ❓ **Conflict** — sources disagree; both values shown.

**Always verify the project RERA filing at <https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/> before transacting.** A registration number alone does not certify that current possession dates, escrow status, or carpet areas match the live filing.

## 6. Due-diligence checklist (Devanahalli plotted developments)

### RERA (Karnataka)

- ✅ Verify the project on <https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/> by registration number — **and confirm the prefix is `PR/` (promoter), not `AG/` (agent)**. Many marketing microsites display agent RERA numbers, which guarantee nothing about the project.
- ⚠️ RERA is mandatory for plotted projects above 500 sq.m **OR** with 8+ units (single-source, verify).
- ✅ The Karnataka RERA filing shows the most recent declared possession date, financial disclosures, and complaint history — read the filing, not the brochure.

### Land status

- ✅ Demand the **DC Conversion certificate** (agricultural-to-residential, signed by Deputy Commissioner).
- ✅ Demand the **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** — at minimum 13 years (one verified source recommended 30 years for safety).
- ⚠️ Devanahalli plots fall under **BIAPPA** (Bangalore International Airport Area Planning Authority). Confirm the BIAPPA layout-approval order is on file. Adarsh Savana and KNS Atharva pages explicitly mention BIAPPA approval.
- ✅ Prefer **DC-converted, BIAPPA-approved, RERA-registered** plots over revenue / gram-panchayat sites.
- ⚠️ Prefer **A-Khata** over B-Khata for bankability and resale (Adarsh Savana page explicitly disclosed A-Khata).

### Site / physical

- ⚠️ Monsoon water-logging window (Jul–Sep); groundwater depth cited at 400–500 ft.
- ⚠️ Plots within 5 km of Devanahalli Wildlife Sanctuary may need forest-department clearance.
- ✅ Verify the survey sketch (Tippani) physically matches plot boundaries on site.

### Financial

- ✅ Confirm bank-loan eligibility (HDFC, SBI, LIC HF approved-project lists are useful indirect title checks).
- ✅ For under-construction projects, confirm **70% escrow** compliance.
- ✅ Budget ~6.6–7% of registration value for Karnataka stamp duty + registration on top of plot price.
