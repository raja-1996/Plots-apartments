## Why Devanahalli?

Devanahalli, in North Bengaluru, is one of the fastest-growing real-estate corridors in the city — anchored by Kempegowda International Airport (KIA), roughly **~15 km** from Devanahalli town centre, and a cluster of large-scale infrastructure and industrial catalysts.

**Key growth drivers**
- ✅ **STRR (NH-948A)** — 280.8 km Satellite Town Ring Road by NHAI under Bharatmala Pariyojana, encircling Bengaluru and connecting 12 satellite towns including Devanahalli.
- ✅ **KIADB Aerospace Park** — ~950–1,000 acres, operational; anchor tenants include Boeing, Airbus, HAL and Rolls-Royce. (Earlier widely-quoted "847 acres" appears outdated.)
- ⚠️ **Aerospace & Defence Park** — sources cite ~1,000–2,000 acres. The often-quoted "3,000 acres" refers to the combined hi-tech / defence / hardware zone, **not** an Aero SEZ alone — the SEZ itself is closer to **250 acres**.
- ✅ **Foxconn Bengaluru facility** — commenced iPhone 17 production in August 2025; ~$2.8 B (~₹25,000 Cr) investment.
- **Devanahalli Business Park** and upcoming **Bengaluru Metro extension** to KIA airport — anchoring long-term office and last-mile demand.

**Price trajectory (Devanahalli plots, indicative)**
- 2019: ~₹2,800 /sq.ft (earlier "₹3,200" looks high vs aggregator history)
- 2026: ₹5,500 – ₹8,000 /sq.ft for premium new plots; peripheral STRR pockets ₹2,800 – ₹4,500 /sq.ft
- ✅ **Plot appreciation: 35–40% between 2022–2025** (widely cited)
- ⚠️ Projected appreciation: 15–20% / year through 2027–28 (forecast; not guaranteed)
- ⚠️ ₹10,000 /sq.ft by 2027 for top-tier projects — **speculative marketing claim**, not a base case.

**Rental yields**
- ⚠️ Raw plots: ~2–3% (limited rental market; primarily land-bank assets).
- ⚠️ Villas / built-up units: ~4–5% (expat / aerospace-park demand pocket).
- The "3–5% on plots" figure quoted by some brokers blurs plots and built villas.

## Due-Diligence Checklist Before Buying

1. **RERA registration** — verify the project's number on https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/ (RERA numbers for Brigade Oasis, Prestige Park Drive, Assetz, Sumadhura Panorama, Chartered Woodpecker are listed above).
2. **Khata** — A-Khata strongly preferred; reputable builders deliver A-Khata.
3. **Title & encumbrance** — 30-year title trace + EC from the sub-registrar.
4. **Statutory approvals** — BMRDA / BIAAPA / DTCP (depending on the survey number's jurisdiction).
5. **Conversion certificate** — agricultural-to-residential DC conversion mandatory.
6. **NA (Non-Agricultural) status** — verify.
7. **Layout sanction** — from the competent local authority.
8. **Possession & registration timeline** — written commitment in the sale agreement.
9. **Maintenance & amenities** — corpus, annual fees, RWA formation timeline.
10. **Resale comparables** — cross-check secondary-market quotes on 99acres / Housing / Magicbricks for the same layout.

## Risk Factors

- Many low-priced "builder" layouts in outer Devanahalli are **agricultural conversions still in progress** — avoid until DC conversion is complete.
- Some peripheral plotted layouts price near ₹2,800 /sq.ft but lack road, drainage, sewerage or clubhouse delivery.
- Several upcoming plotted projects have possession windows in **mid-2026 to 2028** — factor in the construction-risk window if you plan to build immediately.
